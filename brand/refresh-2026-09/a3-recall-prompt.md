# A3 含蓄回弯修订

使用内置 ImageGen；A 原稿为主要造型参考，A2 为过于直接的反例。Rex 的两次反馈分别是原稿看不懂折返，以及 A2 过于直接。

Use case: logo-brand. Refine Review Today logo into a subtle, mature A3.
Two inputs have DIFFERENT roles:
Image 1 (A / RECALL) is the CHOSEN BASELINE and the primary reference. Preserve its compact nearly square R silhouette, mature weight, original wordmark scale, and sheet composition.
Image 2 (A2 / RETURN) is NEGATIVE / OVER-LITERAL REFERENCE ONLY: the user rejected its obvious left arrow and horizontally stretched proportions. Do not copy its arrowhead, its wide shape, or its functional-back-button character.
Goal: something between them. More perceptible returning movement than image 1, but NO literal arrow. This is a brand symbol for thoughtful adult learning and active recall, not a UI command.
Make one precise structural refinement to image 1: the top right R bowl should make a generous smooth 180-degree bend, and its inner lower stroke should travel a short, CLEARLY HORIZONTAL distance BACK TOWARD THE LEFT before ending. Extend the returning end by roughly one stroke-width compared with image 1. Its terminal must be a PLAIN FLAT vertical cut of exactly the same thickness as the stroke; absolutely no pointed tip, no widening, no chevron, no wedge and no triangle. That small leftward segment and the strong rounded turn should suggest a path that comes back inward, without literally telling people to go back.
Keep the logo compact, about equal height and width as in image 1. Keep a generous open central counter, keep the flat return terminal well away from the left stem. The lower-right diagonal R leg is a simple blunt stroke with a crisp diagonal separation from the returning upper bowl. Make the quiet horizontal returning end the important small detail. Avoid a disconnected P plus slash; retain a cohesive, balanced R. Do not convert into a maze, spiral, refresh ring, infinity, e, or G. Do not add any separate shape, eye, dot, arrow, diagram or motion lines.
Imagine a heavy continuous strip curling back into its own open interior, but render it entirely FLAT with one solid ink. No 3D ribbon, folded-paper shading, texture or decorative illustration. Rounded outer shoulder plus restrained flat cut: quiet, intelligent, concise.
Board: OPAQUE warm white #F7F6F2 canvas 1536x1024. Follow image 1 layout: top-left small exact label "A3 / RECALL"; large graphite symbol at upper left; symbol + exact wordmark "Review Today" at upper right; two medium app-icon squares below, near-white with teal symbol and deep teal #246C63 with near-white symbol. All main symbol instances must have the EXACT SAME geometry. NO tiny specimen row. Flat uniform fills, crisp edges, no transparency, shadows, glow, gradients, mottling or texture. No explanatory tagline or diagram. The result should read first as a distinctive R, then reward a second look with its subtle returning path.
